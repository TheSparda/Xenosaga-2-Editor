web · runs on your device

Full save editor — gold and every character's level, HP, stats, and E.S. gear. Opens PCSX2 memory-card images directly (and picks the slot), plus every common single-save export. Everything runs in your browser via Python (Pyodide); your save never leaves the device. Works on desktop and Android. Back up first.

Supports .ps2/.mcd memory-card images (PCSX2), .psu (EMS), .psv (PS3), .sps/.xps (SharkPort), .cbs (CodeBreaker), and .max (AR Max).

Edit the disc directly — every enemy's stats (HP, STR, VIT, EATK, EDEF, DEX, EVA, AGL) and battle rewards (EXP, SP, CP), one-click battle-pacing profiles that retune what the stock→break→boost combo loop costs, and shareable patch files. It also knows the retail values, so it can show exactly how your disc differs from an unmodified one — and put it back. Changes write in place; nothing is uploaded. Desktop Chromium only. Back up first.

Browse the game's data — items, key items, E.S. equipment, and the full bestiary (verified stats & rewards) — extracted from the disc. Read-only; search by name, filter the bestiary by field/boss/E.S., sort it, and export it as CSV.

A newer version is available.
